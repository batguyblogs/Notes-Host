## Hybrid Modeling Techinque
A *hybrid model* combines a physics based structure with a data driven ML component where physics provides skeleton structure and the data fills the gaps.

>[!note]
>The goal of hybrid modelling is to account for the details that physics based modeling misses ( like air resistance, and other minor details that are assumed)
