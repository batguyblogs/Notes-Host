### Instrumentation Amplifier 



It converts change in physical quantity/parameter such as temperature, intensity of light, etc into proportional voltage.

![[Assets/circuit2.webp]]

$$
I = \frac{{V_{01} -V_{02}}}{2R+R_{gain}}
$$
also 
$$
I = \frac{V_{1}-V_{2}}{R_{gain}}
$$
equating the two we get

$$
\frac{{V_{01} -V_{02}}}{2R+R_{gain}} = \frac{V_{1}-V_{2}}{R_{gain}}
$$

$$
\boxed{V_{01} - V_{02} = (V_{1}-V_{2})\left( \frac{2R}{R_{gain}} + 1 \right)}
$$
$$
V_{0}\propto(V_{02}-V_{01})
$$
$$
V_{0}=A(V_{01}-V_{02})
$$
$$
V_{0}=\frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}}(V_{02}-V_{01})
$$
$$
V_{0}=-\frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}}(V_{01}-V_{02})
$$
$$
V_{0} = -\frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}} ((V_{1}-V_{2})\left( \frac{2R}{R_{gain}} + 1 \right))
$$
$$
\boxed{V_{0}=\frac{2R}{R_{gain}}+1 \times V_{2}-V_{1}}
$$
