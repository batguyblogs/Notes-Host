# 12/09/26 Sat 10:40am


### Question 1:
A two tank system has the following parameters

$A_{1}$ = $1m^{2}$
$A_{2}$ = 1$m^{2}$
$R_{2}=R_{1}=2 \ {s}/{m^{2}}$
$q_{in} = 0.5m^{3}/s$

model equation:
$$
q_{12}=\frac{{h_{1}-h_{2}}}{R_{1}} , q_{out}=\frac{h_{2}}{R_{2}}
$$
$$
A_{1}\frac{\delta h_{1}}{\delta t}=q_{in}-q_{12}
$$
$$
A_{2} \frac{\delta h_{2}}{\delta t}=q_{12}-q_{out}
$$
Compute:
1. State equation
2. steady state values
3. eigenvalues
4. stability of the system
5. $h_{1} \& \ h_{2} \  \text{after two euler steps with} \ \Delta T =0.5s$

#### Answer:
Substitue known values into the model equation

$$
\frac{\delta h_{1}}{\delta t}=0.5-\frac{{h_{1}-h_{2}}}{2}
$$
$$
\frac{\delta h_{2}}{\delta t}=\frac{{h_{1}-h_{2}}}{2}-\frac{h_{2}}{2}
$$
1. Now model it into the state equation: 
$$
\begin{bmatrix}
\dot{h_{1}} \\
\dot{h_{2}}
\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
A
\end{bmatrix}_{2\times2}
\begin{bmatrix}
h_{1} \\
h_{2}
\end{bmatrix}
+
\begin{bmatrix}
B
\end{bmatrix}_{2\times1}
\begin{bmatrix}
q_{in}
\end{bmatrix}
$$
$$
\boxed{\dot{h}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
-0.5 & 0.5 \\
0.5 & -1
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
h
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
1 \\
0
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
0.5
\end{bmatrix}}
$$
2. The steady state values of h1, h2
can be found by equating $q_{in}=q_{12}=q_{out}=0.5$

we know from $q_{out}=\frac{h_{2}}{R_{2}}$
$$
0.5 = \frac{h_{2}}{2} \implies h_{2}=1
$$
and from $q_{12}=\frac{{h_{1}-h_{2}}}{R_{1}}$
we get
$$
0.5=\frac{{h_{1}-1}}{2} \implies h_{1}=2
$$
$$
\boxed{h_{1}=2,h_{2}=1}
$$
3.Next for eigenvalues 
$$
|[\lambda I-A]|=0
$$
$$
|\lambda
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
-
\begin{bmatrix}
-0.5 & 0.5 \\
0.5 & -1
\end{bmatrix}
|=0
$$
simplifying that we get
$$
|
\begin{bmatrix}
\lambda+0.5 & -0.5 \\
-0.5 & \lambda+1
\end{bmatrix}
|=0
$$
and the determinant simplifies to
$$
(\lambda+0.5)(\lambda+1)-(-0.5)(-0.5)=0
$$
$$
\implies \lambda^{2}+\frac{3}{2}\lambda+\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{4}=0
$$
$$
\implies \lambda^{2}+\frac{3}{2}\lambda+\frac{1}{4}=0
$$
$$
\lambda = \frac{{-\frac{3}{2}\pm \sqrt{ \frac{9}{4} -1}}}{2}
$$
$$
\implies \frac{{-\frac{3}{2}\pm \sqrt{ \frac{5}{4} }}}{2}=-3\pm \sqrt{ 5 }
$$
$$
\boxed{\lambda=-1.31,-0.19}
$$
4. Since both the values are on the left half plane, the system is stable. 

5. Euler Simulation ($\Delta T=0.5s$) $x_{new}=x_{old}+\Delta t.\frac{{\delta x}}{\delta t}$

| Step | $h_{1}$ | $h_{2}$ | $\frac{\delta h_{1}}{\delta t}=0.5-\frac{{h_{1}-h_{2}}}{2}$ | $\frac{\delta h_{2}}{\delta t}=\frac{{h_{1}-h_{2}}}{2}-\frac{h_{2}}{2}$ | new values of $h_{1},h_{2}$ |
| ---- | ------- | ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| 1    | 0       | 0       | $\frac{\delta h_{1}}{\delta t}$ = 0.5                       | $\frac{\delta h_{2}}{\delta t}$ = 0                                     | $h_{1}=0.25,h_{2}=0$        |
| 2    | 0.25    | 0       | $\frac{\delta h_{1}}{\delta t}$ = 0.5                       | $\frac{\delta h_{2}}{\delta t}$ = 0.125                                 | $h_{1}=0.4375,h_{2}=0.0625$ |
| 3    | 0.4375  | 0.0625  | $\frac{\delta h_{1}}{\delta t}$ = 0.3125                    | $\frac{\delta h_{2}}{\delta t}$ = 0.156                                 | $h_{1}=0.75,h_{2}=0.219$    |
