### Filters
there's 5 
1. low pass
2. high pass
3. band pass
4. band reject / notch filter
5. All pass reject (for the purpose of phase shifting)

### Deriving the Low Pass Filter
$$
V_{A} = \frac{V_{i} \times \frac{1}{SC} }{R + \frac{1}{SC}}
$$
$$
V_{A} = \frac{V_{i}}{SRC+1}
$$
$$
V_{0} = V_{A} \times \left(  1 + \frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}} \right)
$$
$$
V_{0} = V_{i} \left( 1+ \frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}} \right) \left( \frac{1}{SCR +1} \right)
$$
$$
V_{0} = V_{i} \left( 1+\frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}} \right) \left(  \frac{1}{1+j\omega CR} \right)
$$
$$
\frac{V_{0}}{V_{i}} = \left( 1+\frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}} \right) \left( \frac{1}{1+j\omega RC} \right)
$$
$$
|G| = \left( 1+\frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}} \right) \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 1 + \omega^{2} R^{2} C^{2} }}
$$ $$
|G|_{max}^{\omega=0} = 1 + \frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}}
$$
$$
|G|_{min}^{\omega=\infty} = 0
$$
$$
|G|^{\omega=\omega_{c}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2 }}\left( 1+\frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}} \right)
$$

### Steps in Designing a Filter
1. Assume C value = 0.01 $\micro F$ 
2. Find the value of R using $f_{c}=\frac{1}{2\pi RC}$ 
3. From the Maximum gain formula i.e. $1+\frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}}$ by assuming any one of them.

$$
V_{a} = \frac{V_{i}R}{\frac{1}{SC}+R}
$$
$$
V_{a} = \frac{V_{i}R}{1+RCS}
$$
$$
V_{0} = V_{i} \frac{R}{R + \frac{1}{SC}} = V_{i} R \frac{CS}{1+RCS}
$$
$$
\frac{V_{0}}{V_{i}} = |G|=\left( 1+\frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}} \right)\left( \frac{\omega RC}{1+R^{2}C^{2}\omega^{2}} \right)
$$
