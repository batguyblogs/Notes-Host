>[!note]- Lumped and Distributed parameters
>While modelling systems always use lumped parameters
>distributed parameteres are data at each point of the pipe 
>
**Lumped parameters** assume that physical properties like resistance, capacitance, and inductance are concentrated at specific points, ignoring wave propagation and time delays.  This **lumped parameter model** is suitable for **low-frequency circuits** where component dimensions are much smaller than the signal wavelength, allowing the system to be described by **ordinary differential equations (ODEs)**.
>
>**Distributed parameters** assume these properties are spread continuously throughout a structure, such as in **transmission lines** or flexible structures.  This **distributed parameter system** accounts for spatial and temporal variations, requiring **partial differential equations (PDEs)** or delay differential equations to model.  It is essential for **high-frequency circuits** or physical systems where dimensions are comparable to the signal wavelength.
