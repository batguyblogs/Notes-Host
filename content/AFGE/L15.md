# 03/09/26 Thu 8:58am


in the compressed state Hydrogen is used for road transport 

Liquid H2 is used in rockets where the cryogenic complexity is required.

why H2 requires special attention

- Invisible risk
  colorless and odorless, hard to notice
- Easy leakage
  small molecule size increases the permeation and leak probability through joints, seals and fittings.
- Ignition hazard 
  low auto ignition temperature
- Buoyant gas
  It rises quickly: encoles roof spaces and poor ventilation are critical risk
