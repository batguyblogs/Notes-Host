### Applications of Hydrogen Fuel Cell in Mobile and Stationary Engines

##### Anode Reaction
$$
2H_{2} \to 4H^{+} + 4e^{-}
$$
##### Cathode Reaction
$$
O_{2} + 4H^{+}+4e^{-} \to 2H_{2}O 
$$
PEM (proton exchange membrane) stack 

![[Assets/Pasted image 20260803141009.png|394]]

advantages 
1. Higher efficiency compared to IC engine 
2. Zero emissions at the point of use
3. No moving parts in the stack, so quieter

challenges facing fuel cells
1. cost ( materials, labor, economy of scale)
2. Durability (membrane, catalyst)
3. Lack of H2 Infrastructure : H2 is difficult to produce, transport, and store

### Why Challenges Matter?

#### 1. Material Durability
Catalyst, membrane, supports, seals, and, metallic components degrade under electrochemical, thermal, and mechanical stress.

####  2. System Design
Stack architecture, water/thermal control, balance-of-plant, packaging, cost, and controls must be integrated as one system.

#### 3. Safety Management
