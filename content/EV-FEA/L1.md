### Intro to FEA and CAE

#### What multiphysics-based product development for BEV and FCEV means?

**Multiphysics:** the simulation or analysis of products by considering multiple physical phenomena interacting with each other e.g thermal structural fluid vibration electromagnetic etc

product development : engineering process of designing testing and validating components and systems

BEV runs solely on batteries and electric motors 

FCEV uses hydrogen fuel cells to generate electricity to power electric motors.

**FEA:** a numerical method to solve complex real life problems by creating engineeing models with smaller and manageable elements.

**F**inite **E**lement **A**nalysis

##### Application of FEA:
1. Structural analysis
2. Thermal Analysis
3. Fluid analysis
4. Transient analysis

CAE stands for Computer-Aided Engineering.

CAE=FEA+Computer processing and simulation.
Integration of computational tools to support engineering design and analysis.

To evaluate how goods will function in real world scenarios. 

#### Importance of FEA and CAE in engineering
- Reduce physical prototype development time and cost
- Accurate prediction of real-word performance
- failure rate prediction and performance optimisation
- reduced time to market.
