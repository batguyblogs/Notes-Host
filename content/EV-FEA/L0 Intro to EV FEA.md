**Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE)** is a systems engineering methodology that uses graphical models to represent and design entire systems, focusing on requirements, behavior, architecture, and testing to manage complexity and improve collaboration across multidisciplinary teams.  In contrast, **Model-Based Design (MBD)** is a specific engineering methodology that uses mathematical and physical models to design and simulate control systems and dynamic behavior, often generating code directly from these models.
