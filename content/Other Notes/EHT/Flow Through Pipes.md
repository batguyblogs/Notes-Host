## Loss & Energy in Pipes

$$P=Q\times \rho \times g\times h_{L}$$
$P$ is Power, (W J/s)
$Q$ is $AV$ , Discharge or flow-rate, ($m^{3}$/s)
$\rho$ is density,(kg/$m^{3}$)
$g$ is gravitational constant?,
$h_{L}$ is Head loss, (m)

### Dary-Weisbach Formula

$$
h_{f} = \dfrac{4.f.L.V^{2}}{d\times2g}
$$
where $h_{f}$ = loss of head due to friction (m)
f = Darcy-friction factor which is a function of reynolds number
