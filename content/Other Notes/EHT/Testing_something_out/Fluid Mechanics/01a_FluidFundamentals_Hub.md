# Domain Hub — Fluid Fundamentals

This domain addresses the most foundational questions in fluid mechanics: *What exactly is a
fluid?* *How does it differ from a solid?* *What happens when you apply a force to it?* and
*How is the field of fluid mechanics subdivided?* These questions are not merely definitional —
they establish the physical reasoning that underpins every quantitative result in the course.
