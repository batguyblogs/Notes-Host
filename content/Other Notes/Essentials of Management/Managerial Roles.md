Quickly go through Henry Mintzberg's model!

## Interpersonal roles:
1. Figurehead role
2. Leader roles 
3. Liaison role

## Informational roles:
4. Monitor role
5. Disseminator role
6. Spokesman role

## Decisional roles:
7. Entrepreneurial  role
8. Disturbance handler role
9. Resource allocator role 
10. Negotiator role

---
1. Figurehead role
   the face of the unit , has to go places etc etc...
2. Leader Role
   in short leads the unit, is responsible for the direct subordinates
3. Liaison role
   spends time with subordinates and then people outside their chain of command, gets information and builds a personal information network
4. Monitor role
   remember the monitoring by wandering around example sir gave? 
   manager gathers info from their immediate environment and interrogates their liaison and subordinate contacts, to get unsolicited information.
5. Disseminator role
   the manager shares and distributes much of the information that the subordinates would not have access to and can act as a relay of information between subordinates
6. Spokesman role
   the manager sens some of their information to people outside their unit
   in addition they have to satisfy the influential people who control their organizational unit 
7. Entrepreneurial role
   manager is a initiator of change
   seeks to improve and adopt new condition
8. Disturbance handler role
   manager has to all the involuntary responses to pressures
9. Resource allocator role
   they have the responsibility of deciding who will receive what in their organization 
10. Negotiator
    the manager has to keep on top of taking negotiations seriously
