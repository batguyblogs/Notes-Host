Also called ***Business Portfolio Matrix*** ***or the BOSTON BOX*** or the ***PRODUCT PORTFOLIO MATRIX***

Boston Consulting Group developed a techique to classify businesses or products are low or high performers, looking at the parameters **Market Growth Rate** and **Relative Market share**

![[Assets/Pasted image 20260307122220.png|640]]

now yap about these 

Question Marks - BAD get rid of them asap
STARS - upgraded cash cows , but also need more upkeep 
Dogs - risky, can become a cash cow, or can become a question mark
Cash Cows - the best kind, milk them for as much money as you can get and then invest in them till they become a star (when the market grows)

>[!note] Relative Market Share
> measures a business's or product's market position by comparing it's  share to that of its largest competitor.

>[!check] Market Growth Rate
>is the percentage change in the total size of a market ( by revenue, volume, or customers ) over a specific period, usually per year.
