1. **Primary:**
   extraction of raw materials directly from the earth or the sea.  
2. **Secondary:**
   Process and manufacture goods from raw materials.
3. **Tertiary:**
   Industries that provide services.
4. **Quaternary:**
   Industries that incorporate high degree of research and technology in their processes and employ highly qualified people.
