# What is Planning? 
A plan is a blueprint for goal achievement, a blueprint that specifies the necessary resource allocations, schedules, tasks, and other actions to achieve the purpose.

## Why is planning important? : 
- It bridges the gap between where we are & where we want to be.
- Reduces Uncertainties
- Provides Direction
- Resource allocation
- Minimises impulses & arbitrary decisions.

# **Planning answers 6 basic questions** _in regard to any activity:_

- what needs to be accomplished? what are the alternative routes to it?
- when is the deadline? 
- where will this be done? 
- who will be responsible ? 
- how will it get done? 
- how much time, energy, and resources are required to accomplish this goal?


## Types of plans
1. Purpose / Mission / Vision
2. Goal / Objective
3. Strategy
4. Policy
5. Procedure
6. Rules
7. Programs
8. Budgets
