# Urwicks's pricnicples quick summary

1. Unity of Objectives
   organisation is structured well if individuals can contribute to the enterprise's objectives 
2. Organizational Efficiency 
   org is efficient if the structure allows for minimum consequenses or costs to effect the objectives
3. Specialization
   the whole work should be divided amongst subordinates on the basis of qualifications, abilities and skills. 
4. Span of Management
   theres a limit to the number of people one person can manage
5. Scalar Chain
   same as before, continuous line of superiors through whom information/ commands should be relayed
6. Unity of Command
   exact same as heri fayol's
7. Delegation
   authority must be delegated as far down the chain as possible 
8. Parity of authority and responsibility
   similar to fayol but as a rule not just an observation
9. Flexibility
   the org's structure needs to be simple to understand but also flexible. 
10. Functional Definition
    every position and department in the org must have a clear definition of the results expected.
