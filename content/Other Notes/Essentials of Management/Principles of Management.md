These are quick notes to recollect quickly what to relate each of the 14 principles to.

## 1. Division of Labour

subdivision of work leads to efficiency
specialization leads to efficiency
this leads to profits

## 2. Authority & responsibility 

authority without responsibility and vice versa will cause problems.

## 3. Unity of Command, 4. Unity of Direction

refer [[Other Notes/Essentials of Management/Unity of Command Vs Unity of Direction]]

## 5. Equity
managers should treat their subordinates in a fair kind and just manner
although this doesnt mean they cant be harsh.

## 6. Order
keep people and articles in an assigned easy to query place!

## 7. Discipline
come on you know what discipline is just yap

## 8. Initiative 

~~roll for initiative~~  management should encourage employees to show initiative in bringing new ideas experiences and feedback.

## 9. Fair Remuneration
pay the workers well 
based on factors like cost of living, work assigned, financial position of the business, minimum wage etc

also consider non monetary benefits like education , medical and residential care
## 10. Stability of Tenure
dont move people around too much , in an organisation, they cant work to their best potential

## 11. Scalar Chain

everything has to come from the proper "**channel**" 
but this need not be rigid, temporary measures should be allowed

## 12. Subordination of individual interest to general interest

individual must sacrifice their own goals for the benefit of the entire organization

but the org should try to reconcile with the individual

## 13. Esprit De' Corps
Team spirit 
the org and the managers need to make a huge effort 
to create a sense of belongingness among members and encourage them to form informal bonds b/w each other. 


## 14. Centralization & Decentralization

more work for subordinate is Decentralization
less work for subordinate is Centralization.
