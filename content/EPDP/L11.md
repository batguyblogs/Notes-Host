## Heat Sinks

A transistor with $V_{c}=20V$ and $I_{c}=1A$ has a $1\celsius$ per watt junction to case thermal resistance. If the value of $Q_{cs}=0.4\celsius$ calculate the thermal resistance for the heatsink that will keep the maximum junction temperature at $90\celsius$ when the ambient temperature is $25^{\circ}C$.

soln:
$P_{Dmax}=20\times1=20$
$$
Q_{SA}+Q_{JC}+Q_{CS}=\frac{T_{J}-T_{A}}{P_{Dmax}}
$$

$$
\boxed{Q_{SA}+1+0.4=\frac{90-25}{20}\leadsto Q_{SA}=1.85}
$$

A Power transistor has a thermal resistance of $200\celsius per \  watt$ calculate the maximum permissible power dissipation when the $T_{Jmax}=90\celsius$ and $T_{A}=25\celsius$

$$
\boxed{\frac{90-25}{200}=0.325}
$$
