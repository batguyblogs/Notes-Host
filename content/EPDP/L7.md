# Electronic Product Finishing

1. Cleaning Process
    - Removes flux residues and contaminants.
    - Prepares the board for surface finishing.
2. Surface Finishing 
    - Protects copper traces.
    - Ensures solderability and performance.
    - Common finishes:
        - HASL (Hot Air Solder Leveling)
        - ENIG (Electroless Nickel Immersion Gold)
        - OSP (Organic Solderability Preservative)
        - Immersion Tin/Silver
3. Silkscreen Printing
    - Prints text, labels, component IDs.
    - Uses non conductive ink.
4. Solder Mask application
    - Protects copper from oxidation and solder bridging.
    - Usually green but other colors are also used
5. Testing and inspection
    - Visual Inspection
    - AOI (Automated optical inspection)
    - ICT (In-Circuit Testing)
    - Flying Probe and Functional Testing
