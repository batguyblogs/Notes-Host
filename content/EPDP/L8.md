#### Temperature Testing
- Purpose:
  To ensure the product operates correctly across its specified temperature range ( -40 to +85 for industrial equipment).
- Types:
    - High-Temperature Test: Evaluates performance under prolonged heat.
    - Low - temp: Ensures operation in cold conditions.
    - Thermal cycling / shock: Alternates rapidly between high and low temperatures to test thermal fatigue.
