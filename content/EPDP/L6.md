#### Introduction to Electronic Product Detailing
it refers to the comprehensive process of refining and specifying every aspect of an electronic product's design before it moves to manufacturing. This stage ensures that the product is functional, manufacturable, reliable, and compliant with standards.

#### Key considerations in component selection
electrical specs 
functional
environmental friendly

### workflow for circuit design
- Define Requirements
- Draw Schematic
- Simulate circuit
- Analyze Result
- Iterate and optimize
- Export Netlist

#### Benefits of Using simulation tools
- early fault detection before physical prototyping
- Cost savings by reducing trial and error
- performance optimization through parameter tuning
- safety assurance by testing under extreme conditions
