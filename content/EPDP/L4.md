### What Is Electronic Product Planning?
- Strategic process of transforming ideas into electronic products
- Involved Technical, market, and manufacturing planning
- Crucial for innovation and business success

#### Stages in Product Planning
1. Idea generation
2. feasibility study
3. specification development
4. 
5. design & prototyping
6. Testing and Validation
7. Manufacturing plan
8. Product launch

#### Market Research and Identifying Needs
- Understand customer problems and gaps in the market 
- Methods: surveys, interviews, Online trends
- Example: Demand for affordable IoT home devices

#### Feasibility Study
- Technical: Can we **build** it?
- Financial: Is it **Profitable**?
- Legal: Are there **regulatory barriers**?
- Environmental: Are we being **eco-friendly?**

#### Prototyping and Validation
- Build MVP (minimum viable product)
- breadboard or custom pcb prototypes
- test, emi, power, duraibtity, thermal
- use feedback for iteration


#### Manufacturing and Production Planning
- BOM ( bill of materials )
- selecting components and suppliers
- SMT/THT (surface mount / through hole)
- QC (quality control) assembly line setup

#### Product Launch & Life Cycle
- Launch strategy ( online retail beta)
- collect user feedback
- Plan updates or next-gen product
- Manage end - of life ( EOL ) and recycling.
