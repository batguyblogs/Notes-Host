Electronic packaging
- Electronic packaging is the design and production of enclosures for electronic devices ranging from individual semiconductor devices up to complete systems such as a mainframe computer.
- Packaging of an electronic system must consider protection from mechanical damage cooling RF noise emission and electrostatic discharge.
- Product safety standards may dictate particular features of a consumer product.
- Packaging for aerospace marine or military systems imposes different types of design criteria.
