Intro to system engineering

importance - > system requirements define measurable targets for design and validation

**example ev power train requirements**
- 0 -100 km/h acceleration $<$ 10 secs
- battery range $\ge$ 400 km per charge

**Requirements help in:** 
bench-marking performance
ensuring component compatibility


trade-offs in system engineering

efficiency vs cost
performance vs weight
safety vs manufacturability

a well designed set of system requirement ensures an optimal balance.


#### Understanding an EV as a system

an EV consists of multiple interconnected components, including:
- battery pack
- moter & motor controller
- Power Electrinics
- thermal managemnet system 
- brakign system 
- chassis & sensors 
- communication networks 

System Boundary of an EV
- the vehicle itself forms the system boundary

#### The System Perspective: Beyond Individual Components
- Analyzing **individual components alone is not enough** to predict full vehicle behaviour
- Subsystem interactions can cause **emergent behaviours** -- Unexpected effect not seen in isolation. 
- A **holistic approach** ensures seamless integration and optimal operation

![[Assets/Pasted image 20260730105526.png]]

***Illustrative Examples:***
**Larger Battery**
- Increases driving range
- Adds weight -> impacts *acceleration, braking, and structural intergrity*
**Regenerative Braking**
- Improves energy efficiency
- Must be balanced with *mechanical braking* for safety & stability across conditions
- Systems thinking ensures EV design is not just optimal at component level, but *coherent, safe, and efficient* as a whole.
##### System Success vs. System Failure
- **Success:**
    - Desired behaviour *emerges as expected*
- **Failure occurs when:**
    - *Expected behavior doesn't occur*
        - E.x, EV expected to run 300 km only acheives 250 km due to unforseen power losses
    - *Unexpected, undesirable behaviour occurs*
        - E.g, Thermal-electrical interaction causing battery overheating

#### Introduction to System Engineering Process
- After grasping the **definition and approach of systems engineering**, the next step is to understand its step is to understand its **structured process**
- This methodology ensures a **Logical, efficient development cycle** for complex systems like EV powertrains
- ***Why it matters:***
  Guides engineers through **requirement definition, design, integration and validation**
- Ensures each phase is aligned with **system objectives**, performance, and reliability needs
