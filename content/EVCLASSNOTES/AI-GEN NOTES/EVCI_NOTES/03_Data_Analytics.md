# Data Analytics and Challenges

**Data analytics in EVCI is used to predict charger failures, optimize energy pricing, and forecast future demand patterns.**

## Key Challenges

1. **Data Silos:** Different hardware manufacturers use proprietary data formats, making it hard to aggregate data in a central CSMS. (Solved by **OCPP**).
2. **Connectivity Latency:** Stations in underground parking or rural highways often have poor 4G/5G signals, leading to gaps in real-time telemetry.
3. **Data Privacy:** Managing User Charging Behavior data (where you charge, when, and how much) requires strict compliance with data protection laws (GDPR/DPDP).

## Why It Matters

**Predictive Maintenance:** Analytics can detect a rising temperature trend in a power module *before* it fails. This allows the CPO to send a technician with a replacement part before the station actually goes offline, maintaining high user trust.
