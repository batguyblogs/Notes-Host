# Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure (EVCI) — Master Hub

**A comprehensive knowledge base covering the end-to-end ecosystem of Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure, from power electronics and international standards to grid integration and lifecycle economics.**

## Topic Map

- **[[EVCLASSNOTES/AI-GEN NOTES/EVCI_NOTES/01a_EVCI_Systems_Hardware_Hub\|Domain A: Systems & Power Hardware]]**
	- Power converters (AC/DC, DC/DC), switching devices, and modular station architecture.
- **[[EVCLASSNOTES/AI-GEN NOTES/EVCI_NOTES/01b_EVCI_Communication_Standards_Hub\|Domain B: Communication & Standards]]**
	- CP/PP signaling, CCS protocols (ISO 15118), and regulatory frameworks (IEC/SAE).
- **[[EVCLASSNOTES/AI-GEN NOTES/EVCI_NOTES/01c_EVCI_Grid_Infrastructure_Hub\|Domain C: Grid & Distribution Infrastructure]]**
	- Utility planning, urban network design, and supply options (Renewable/Captive).
- **[[EVCLASSNOTES/AI-GEN NOTES/EVCI_NOTES/01d_EVCI_Safety_Economics_Operations_Hub\|Domain D: Safety, Economics & Operations]]**
	- Insulation monitoring, TCO methodology, charging management systems (CSMS), and roaming.

## How the Domains Connect

The EVCI ecosystem is fundamentally interdependent. **Hardware** choices (Domain A) are dictated by **Standards** (Domain B) to ensure interoperability. The resulting power demand dictates the **Grid Infrastructure** (Domain C) requirements, while the entire deployment is made viable through robust **Safety** protocols and **Economic** models (Domain D).
