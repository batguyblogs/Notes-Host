# Site Planning Guidelines

**Effective site planning balances accessibility, utilization, and infrastructure cost to ensure a safe and efficient user experience.**

## Key Layout Dimensions

### 1. Parking Bay Width
The standard width for an EV parking spot is **2.5 meters**.
- **Side-mounted chargers:** Require **2.75–3.0m** to allow cable handling without door-dinging adjacent cars.
- **Accessible (Disabled) bays:** Require **3.6m** plus a transfer zone.

### 2. Aisle and Clearance
- **Aisle Width:** Minimum **6.0m** for two-way traffic.
- **Overhead Clearance:** Minimum **2.3m** (passenger) or **4.5m** (bus/truck).

### 3. Cable Management
Chargers should be positioned to minimize cable length. Cables longer than 5 meters require retractors or specialized management to prevent trip hazards and mechanical wear from being run over by vehicles.

## Why It Matters

### Maximizing Utilization
Poorly planned sites (e.g., chargers located in corners that are hard to maneuver into) result in "Ghost Downtime" where a charger is functional but unused because a car cannot reach it easily.

### Safety & Liability
Inadequate spacing leads to user injury or vehicle damage. Proper grounding and lighting are mandatory to prevent theft and ensure safety during nighttime charging.

> [!warning] Exam Trap
> Don't forget **Drive-Away Prevention**. Site planning should include physical barriers (bollards) to protect the expensive DCFC cabinets from accidental vehicle impact.
