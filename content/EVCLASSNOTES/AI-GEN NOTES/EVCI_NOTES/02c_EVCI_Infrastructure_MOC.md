# Grid & Infrastructure MOC

**This layer synthesizes the macroscopic challenges of integrating a massive new load (EVs) into the existing electrical distribution network.**

## Thematic Clusters

### 1. Grid Impacts & Stress Factors
Massive EV adoption is not just a load growth problem; it is a power quality and demand coincidence problem.
> [!info]
> - [[EVCLASSNOTES/AI-GEN NOTES/EVCI_NOTES/03_Grid_Distribution_Impacts]] — Transformer overloading, voltage drop, and harmonics.
> - [[EVCLASSNOTES/AI-GEN NOTES/EVCI_NOTES/03_Demand_Coincidence_and_Peak_Shaving]] — Why STATCOMS and smart charging are needed to defer upgrades.

### 2. Supply & Metering Options
How power reaches the station dictates its cost, reliability, and carbon footprint.
> [!info]
> - [[EVCLASSNOTES/AI-GEN NOTES/EVCI_NOTES/03_Electricity_Supply_Options]] — Existing vs New connections vs Captive Renewables.
> - [[EVCLASSNOTES/AI-GEN NOTES/EVCI_NOTES/03_Net_and_Minus_Metering]] — Facilitating V2G through bidirectional billing.

### 3. Practical Site Planning
Designing an urban hub requires balancing vehicle throughput, electrical capacity, and accessibility.
> [!info]
> - [[EVCLASSNOTES/AI-GEN NOTES/EVCI_NOTES/03_Urban_Charging_Network_Planning]] — Design calculation for 2W/3W/4W mixed hubs.
> - [[EVCLASSNOTES/AI-GEN NOTES/EVCI_NOTES/03_Site_Planning_Guidelines]] — Parking bay dimensions, accessibility, and cable management.

### 4. Wireless Infrastructure
Wireless charging represents the next frontier, especially for autonomous fleets and heavy-duty en-route charging.
> [!info]
> - [[EVCLASSNOTES/AI-GEN NOTES/EVCI_NOTES/03_Inductive_Charging_Static_Dynamic]] — Resonant coupling, road-embedded coils, and ERS.

## Cross-Cluster Connections

- **[[EVCLASSNOTES/AI-GEN NOTES/EVCI_NOTES/03_Grid_Distribution_Impacts]] ↔ [[EVCLASSNOTES/AI-GEN NOTES/EVCI_NOTES/03_Electricity_Supply_Options]]**: Captive renewables with BESS (Option 3) act as a buffer, mitigating the transformer stress (Impact 1).
- **[[EVCLASSNOTES/AI-GEN NOTES/EVCI_NOTES/03_Urban_Charging_Network_Planning]] ↔ [[EVCLASSNOTES/AI-GEN NOTES/EVCI_NOTES/03_Inductive_Charging_Static_Dynamic]]**: Future urban hubs may replace physical connectors with static inductive pads for taxi ranks.

## Open Problems / Gaps
- Equitable access to charging in low-income neighborhoods with weak distribution grids.
- Standardization of "Wayside Amenities" for electric heavy-duty vehicles (HDEVs).
