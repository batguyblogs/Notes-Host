# Indian Standards (BIS IS 17017)

**The IS 17017 series, published by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), defines the technical and safety requirements for EV charging in the Indian context.**

## Key Parts of IS 17017

### IS 17017-1: Basic Requirements
Derived from IEC 61851-1. It defines the charging modes (1-4) and general safety requirements suitable for Indian grid conditions.

### IS 17017-2 (Connectors)
Defines the physical connector types permitted in India:
- **Type-2 (AC):** Standard for single and three-phase charging.
- **CCS-2 (DC):** The mandated standard for public fast charging.
- **CHAdeMO:** Permitted but declining.

### IS 17017-23 (DC Charging)
Provides the specific safety and testing requirements for Public Charging Stations (PCS).

## The "Bharat" Standards
Before the full adoption of IS 17017, India deployed specific low-cost standards:
- **Bharat AC-001:** 10 kW (3 outputs of 3.3 kW each, Type-2 sockets).
- **Bharat DC-001:** 15 kW (GB/T connector, 72V-200V range).

## Why It Matters

### Grid Resilience
Indian standards include specific provisions for protection against the high voltage fluctuations and frequent transients common in local distribution grids.

### Localization
Standardization allows Indian manufacturers to produce components (chargers, connectors, controllers) that are guaranteed to work across all domestic network operators.

> [!caution] Time-Sensitive
> As of 2024-2025, the **Bharat DC-001** is being phased out for high-power public stations in favor of **CCS-2 (minimum 50 kW)** to align with global automotive trends. Check the latest Ministry of Power (MoP) guidelines for current tender requirements.
