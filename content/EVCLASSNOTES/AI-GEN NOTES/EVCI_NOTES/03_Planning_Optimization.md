# Planning Optimization and Constraints

**EV infrastructure planning uses mathematical optimization to balance conflicting goals such as maximizing accessibility while minimizing grid upgrade costs.**

## Key Concepts

### 1. The Objective Function
A mathematical expression defining the goal of the plan.
- **Example A:** Maximize the number of EVs served per hour (Utilization).
- **Example B:** Minimize the distance between the user and the nearest charger (Accessibility).

### 2. Constraints
Real-world limits that the optimization must respect.
- **Grid Constraints:** Maximum kVA capacity of the local transformer.
- **Land Constraints:** Physical parking spot availability and zoning laws.
- **Budget Constraints:** Maximum allowable CAPEX per site.

## Why It Matters

Without optimization, charging hubs are often placed in "Convenient" locations that turn out to be "Grid Weak" spots. This leads to high **Interconnection Costs** that can kill the project's financial viability.
