# Maintenance and Inspections

**Regular maintenance of EV charging infrastructure is a multi-dimensional risk mitigation strategy addressing safety, reliability, and regulatory compliance.**

## Three Key Reasons for Maintenance

### 1. Electrical Safety Assurance
- **Insulation Degradation:** Cables age due to UV exposure and mechanical stress. Leakage currents can cause shock hazards.
- **Thermal Cycling:** High-current terminals heat up and cool down, leading to loose connections. Loose terminals = high resistance = **Fire Risk**.

### 2. Operational Uptime (Revenue)
- **Mechanical Wear:** Connector pins (IEC 62196) are typically rated for **10,000 mating cycles**. Bent pins or cracked housings lead to charging failures.
- **Filter Cleaning:** Power modules in DCFC stations pull large volumes of air; clogged filters lead to module overheating and shutdown.

### 3. Regulatory and Warranty Compliance
- Failure to perform scheduled servicing often **voids manufacturer warranties**.
- Many national codes (e.g., IS 3043 for grounding) mandate periodic testing of earth electrode resistance (Target: **< 1 $\Omega$**).

## Why It Matters

Preventive maintenance converts **Emergency Repairs** (expensive, downtime) into **Planned Servicing** (budgeted, high availability). For a CPO, "Uptime" is the single most important metric for profitability and user trust.
