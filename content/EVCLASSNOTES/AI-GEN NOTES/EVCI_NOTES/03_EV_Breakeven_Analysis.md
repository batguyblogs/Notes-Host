# EV Breakeven Analysis

**The breakeven point is the cumulative distance at which the total lifecycle cost of an EV equals that of an ICE vehicle.**

## The Breakeven Formula ($D^*$)

$$D^* = \frac{C_{acq,EV} - C_{acq,ICE}}{\text{Running Cost Advantage per km}}$$

*Where the denominator is the difference in per-km energy and maintenance costs.*

### Impact of Daily Running Distance
The breakeven point in **Years** is highly sensitive to how much the vehicle is driven daily:
- **10 km/day:** Breakeven may take 7+ years (often exceeding the battery warranty).
- **100 km/day:** Breakeven achieved in **under 12 months**.

## Why It Matters

### Commercial vs Private Adoption
This analysis explains why **Commercial Fleets (Taxis, Last-mile delivery)** are electrifying faster than private car owners. Their high daily mileage "compresses" the payback period, making the switch an immediate economic win.

### Policy Sensitivity
If petrol prices rise, the breakeven distance $D^*$ decreases. If electricity tariffs for EV charging are set too high (commercial vs domestic rates), the payback period lengthens, potentially stalling adoption.

> [!caution] Time-Sensitive
> These figures are based on 2024-2026 fuel and battery prices. As battery prices fall at a CAGR of ~10%, the breakeven point moves closer to 0 km, eventually reaching **Price Parity** where the EV is cheaper even on Day 1.
