# HDEV Charging Scenarios

**Heavy-Duty Electric Vehicles (HDEVs) like buses and trucks require specialized charging scenarios due to their massive battery packs (200-800 kWh) and high utilization rates.**

## Comparison of Scenarios

| Scenario | Power Level | Impact on Grid | Practical Application |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Depot Overnight** | 50-150 kW | **Low.** Managed during off-peak hours. | City bus fleets, urban delivery trucks. |
| **En-route Fast** | 350-1000 kW | **High.** Concentrated demand at highway nodes. | Long-haul trucking during mandatory driver breaks. |
| **Opportunity** | 150-300 kW | **Medium.** Frequent short bursts. | Delivery vans during loading/unloading at terminals. |

## Innovative Solutions

### 1. Battery Swapping
Replacing a depleted battery with a full one in < 5 minutes.
- **Advantage:** Decouples charging time from vehicle operation.
- **Challenge:** Requires extreme standardization of chassis and battery packs.

### 2. Electric Road Systems (ERS)
Continuous power delivery while in motion via overhead catenary wires or road-embedded coils.
- **Advantage:** Allows for much smaller, lighter batteries.
- **Challenge:** Enormous infrastructure CAPEX (€1-3M per km).

## Why It Matters

A single 40-tonne electric truck fast-charging at 350 kW draws as much power as **50 households**. Managing HDEV charging is not just a hardware problem, but a macroscopic grid balancing challenge.
