An **electric vehicle** (EV) is a motorized vehicle propelled mostly by electric power. 
EVs encompass **road** (*cars, buses, trucks, and personal transporters* ( *two wheelers* ) ), **rail**, **boats** , and the list goes on. 

But for the purposes of this course we focus on **Electric Cars** for *dynamics*. and all **the road vehicles** for the *EV market*.
